ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why This Tool?

Internship Scam Detector is a web-based project designed to help students identify common warning signs in internship offers.

The application analyzes the information entered by the user and generates a simple risk score.

⚠️ This is only a first-level check. Always verify the company independently.
